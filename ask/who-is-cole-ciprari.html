Cole Ciprari is a Business Systems Architect based in Worcester, Massachusetts. Earlier roles span Hannaford Bros., Andyman Dessert Baking, Naples Pizzeria (Managing Partner, $1.2M P&L), TSD Rental and Liberty Mutual, where he built RPA pipelines and a 200+ process knowledge base serving 3,000+ users.

He is Claude Code Certified (Anthropic) and an OpenAI Certified Architect. His résumé is an interactive operating system at ciprari.ai, he writes at the changelog, and he's on LinkedIn at linkedin.com/in/coleos.
