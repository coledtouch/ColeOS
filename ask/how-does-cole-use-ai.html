How does Cole Ciprari use AI in production systems?

short answer: AI drafts, humans approve: estimating, scope drafting, document generation and agents — always behind a human review gate before anything reaches a system of record.

Cole ships AI where it earns its keep: AI estimating (SOW/MTO) in the Coen Construction ERP, per-line-confidence scope drafting in estimate.pro, MLS copy generation in curbscript.com, and an AI advisor in thepunchlist.ai. ColeOS itself runs ColeAI, a Claude-powered assistant, against a Cloudflare Workers backend.

The architecture rule is constant: nothing an AI produces writes to a system of record without a human approving it. Drafts, suggestions and generated documents flow freely; commits are gated. The reasoning — and the failure stories that justify it — are documented on the changelog, his AI/tech engineering blog.
